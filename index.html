▸ The problem

The hard part isn't the paperwork. It's the agreement.

How much equity for each of you, what vesting, what happens if someone leaves, who breaks a tie. Today that lives in scattered conversations, a document nobody understands, and expensive lawyer hours —and nobody ends up with a clear record of what was agreed. Years later, in a round or a co-founder dispute, that ambiguity is exactly what breaks cap tables and companies.

WhatsApp threads A confusing Google Doc Three private AIs with no shared truth Emails with version numbers Expensive lawyer hours Zero decision record
▸ How it works

From conversation to seal.

▸ STEP 01
Tell us about your case

Five questions in plain language: how many co-founders, whether you'll raise capital, how the split will work. Two minutes, no legal jargon.

▸ STEP 02
Get your decision agenda

Circule builds the exact list of decisions your case needs, with what's typically done for each one, and flags where you need to agree.

▸ STEP 03
Decide and seal it

Close each decision with the facilitator and your private copilot. The document is sealed clause by clause. At the end: case file, minutes, and signature.

▸ IN PARALLEL
Each co-founder has a copilot

A private AI per person, explaining each proposal in plain terms and preparing counter-proposals. No one else sees what you ask.

▸ Why Circule

The agreement gets recorded, not just written.

Decisions, not just text changes

Every agreement records who approved it, when, and with what caveats. A record that holds up in due diligence or a dispute.

A neutral facilitator

Flags what's pending, contradictions, and risks. And every co-founder has a private copilot working for their own interest.

People decide, AI helps

The agents propose and explain. Only you and your authorized co-founders can approve or sign. Never the AI on your behalf.

Connects to the filing

Circule prepares the agreement and hands it off to your incorporation platform. It doesn't replace your lawyer, notary, or registry.

Fast platforms solve the paperwork. Circule solves the agreement that comes before it.

We don't compete with Clerky or Stripe Atlas: we connect to them. They file the paperwork once you've decided; we're the room where you decide.
